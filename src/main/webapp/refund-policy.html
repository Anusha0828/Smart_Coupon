Buyer Protection

Refund & Dispute Policy

Effective Date: November 14, 2026
The 24-Hour CouponLoop Guarantee: Every purchase made on CouponLoop is covered by our full refund guarantee in the event that an unlocked coupon code cannot be redeemed.
1. Eligible Grounds for Refund
  • The coupon code was already redeemed prior to your purchase.
  • The merchant platform rejects the code as "invalid" or "non-existent".
  • The coupon was bound to the seller's specific account or mobile number.
  • The coupon expiry date occurred before the date stated in the listing.
2. Non-Refundable Scenarios
  • Buyer change of mind after the secret coupon code has been revealed.
  • Failure to meet the stated minimum cart requirement clearly detailed in the listing.
  • Allowing the coupon to expire after the stated valid expiry window.
3. Dispute Process & Turnaround

To raise a claim, go to your Buyer Dashboard → My Purchases → click Report / Dispute. Provide details of the merchant error message. Our administrative team will review the dispute within 24 hours. Upon approval, the transaction will be marked refunded and the seller earning cancelled.
