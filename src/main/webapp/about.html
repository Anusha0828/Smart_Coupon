Our Mission

Turning Unused Coupons into Value

Bridging the gap between people with unneeded promotional vouchers and shoppers eager for great savings.

The Problem We Solve

Every day, millions of people receive promotional vouchers, discount codes, and partner passes from payment apps like Google Pay, PhonePe, and Paytm, or during online shopping and food delivery. A large portion of these eligible promotional vouchers are never redeemed simply because the recipient doesn't require the specific service at that moment, forgets about it, or lets it expire.

Meanwhile, other shoppers are about to book a flight, order food, or purchase apparel and are actively looking for working coupons.

The CouponLoop Solution

CouponLoop creates a secure digital marketplace where sellers monetize unused transferable vouchers, buyers unlock deep savings, and merchant promotions reach truly interested customers without code waste.

Value for Sellers

Recover real cash from promotional vouchers that would otherwise expire uselessly.

Savings for Buyers

Discover verified, deep discounts from major brands with 24-hr dispute protection.

Reach for Brands

Help promotional campaigns actually drive orders with high-intent new customers.

Our Transferability Commitment

We respect merchant terms and digital commerce guidelines. Direct UPI cashback rewards bound to personal bank accounts are prohibited. Only transferable voucher codes with third-party redemption rights are allowed on CouponLoop.
