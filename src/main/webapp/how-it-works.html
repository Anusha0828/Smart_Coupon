How CouponLoop Works

A step-by-step walkthrough of our secure voucher exchange mechanism.

Buyer Experience

Discovering & Redeeming Deals

Step 1: Browse & Filter

Search hundreds of deals by brand (Swiggy, Zomato, Myntra, etc.), category, price range, or expiring urgency.

Step 2: Checkout via Sandbox

Review coupon terms and pay securely via demo UPI, card, or netbanking. Our transactional engine prevents double-purchase.

Step 3: Instant Unlock

The voucher code is instantly revealed with one-click copy. Redeem on merchant app, backed by our 24-hr dispute guarantee.

Seller Experience

Monetizing Unneeded Rewards

Step 1: Upload Coupon

Enter voucher details, face value, and code. Confirm eligibility and transferability under issuer terms.

Step 2: Smart Price Guidance

Our algorithmic pricing suggestion analyzes days to expiry and brand popularity to recommend an optimal competitive price.

Step 3: Receive Payout

When bought, you earn 90% net of the 10% platform commission. Withdraw directly to your active UPI handle.
