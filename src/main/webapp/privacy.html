Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 14, 2026
1. Information We Collect

CouponLoop collects account information (full name, email address, mobile number), transaction history, and payout UPI addresses. We never request or store sensitive banking PINs, payment app logins, or OTPs.

2. Password Security

All passwords are cryptographically salted and hashed using SHA-256 before being stored in MySQL. Plaintext passwords are never recorded in logs or persistent storage.

3. Voucher Code Secrecy

Coupon codes provided by sellers are masked with cryptographic protection and only revealed to the authenticated buyer following confirmed payment verification.

4. Cookies & Session Storage

We use secure HTTP-only cookies solely for session continuity and role-based authentication. We do not sell user behavioral data to third-party advertising brokers.
